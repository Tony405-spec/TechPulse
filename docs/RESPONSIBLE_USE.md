# Responsible use and limitations

TechPulse is an academic decision-support and research tool. It does not make investment, hiring, procurement, technology-migration, or guaranteed career decisions.

- Community question activity is platform participation, not total usage, software quality, or a direct measure of Kenyan developer interest.
- Survey usage, interest, and admiration shares are respondent-sample measures, not sentiment. The supplied aggregate is global/unspecified.
- A company profile is not proof of a technology stack. Generated adoption and question-company mappings are proxies and must not appear as observed evidence.
- GitHub stars, registry downloads, releases, and sampled question counts describe public-source snapshots; they do not establish unique developers or production use.
- Kenya-specific jobs, training outcomes, company evidence, and regional activity are unavailable in the supplied data. NULL/unavailable is not zero.
- Predictions and attributions are uncertain associations. Show data coverage, missingness, label support, model run timestamp, split dates, and calibration status with any probability.
- A high metric on a weak, old, or insufficiently supported test set does not establish reliability or future performance.

Before publication or operational use, verify source terms, sampling coverage, temporal comparability, label validity, class support, calibration, and independent reproducibility.
